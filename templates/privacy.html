Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and what rights you have regarding your information.
1

Data We Collect

When you use HumanizeAI, we may collect the following categories of information:

Category Examples Purpose
Account data Name, email, password hash Account management
Usage data API calls, word counts, timestamps Billing, rate limiting
Technical data IP address, browser, device OS Security, analytics
Payment data Billing address, last 4 digits Subscription processing
Submitted content Text sent for humanization Service delivery only

We do not collect sensitive personal data such as government IDs, biometrics, or financial account credentials. Payment card details are handled entirely by our payment processor (Stripe) and never stored on our servers.

2

How We Use Data

We use the data we collect for the following purposes:

  • Delivering and operating the HumanizeAI service and API
  • Processing payments and managing your subscription plan
  • Enforcing rate limits, detecting abuse, and preventing fraud
  • Sending transactional emails such as receipts, alerts, and password resets
  • Analyzing aggregated, anonymized usage patterns to improve our platform
  • Complying with legal obligations and responding to lawful requests

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

3

Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure (AWS / GCP) within protected environments. We implement the following security measures:

  • Encryption at rest using AES-256 for all stored data
  • Encryption in transit using TLS 1.3 for all API and web communications
  • Token-based authentication (JWT) with short expiry windows
  • Rate limiting, IP-based throttling, and input sanitization in our Python backend
  • Access controls restricting production data to authorized personnel only
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments

While we take all reasonable steps to protect your data, no method of internet transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but are committed to industry best practices.

4

Third-Party Services

We work with trusted third-party providers to operate our service. Each provider is bound by a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) and their own privacy policies:

  • Stripe — payment processing and subscription management
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) / Google Cloud Platform (GCP) — cloud hosting and data storage
  • Analytics providers — aggregate, anonymized usage analysis to improve the platform
  • Email delivery services — sending transactional and system emails

We do not share personally identifiable information with any third party beyond what is strictly necessary to deliver the Service.

5

Content Processing

Text you submit for humanization is processed transiently through our AI pipeline to produce the transformed output. We do not permanently store your submitted content unless:

  • You explicitly save it to your history or dashboard within the app
  • It is retained temporarily (maximum 7 days) for the purpose of debugging a reported error

We do not use your submitted content to train, fine-tune, or improve our AI models without your explicit, informed, opt-in consent. You will always be notified before any such use occurs and will have the option to decline.

6

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website and dashboard. The types of cookies we use are:

  • Essential cookies — required for authentication, session management, and core functionality. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how users interact with the platform (anonymized). You may opt out via your account settings or browser preferences.

We do not use advertising cookies, retargeting cookies, or cross-site tracking technologies. You can manage your cookie preferences at any time in your browser settings or our cookie settings panel.

7

Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable data protection laws (including GDPR for EU/UK users and CCPA for California residents), you have the following rights:

Access
Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
Correction
Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
Deletion
Request deletion of your account and associated personal data.
Portability
Export your data in a portable format (JSON or CSV).
Opt-out
Unsubscribe from non-essential emails and analytics.
Complaint
Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@humanizeai.app. We will respond within 30 days of receiving a verified request.

8

Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Account data — retained while your account is active, plus 90 days after deletion to handle disputes or chargebacks
  • Usage and log data — retained for up to 12 months for abuse prevention, then permanently deleted or anonymized
  • Submitted content — not stored after processing unless saved by you; debugging copies deleted within 7 days
  • Payment records — retained for 7 years as required by financial regulations

After the applicable retention period, data is securely and permanently deleted from our systems.

9

Children's Privacy

HumanizeAI is not directed at, and does not knowingly collect personal data from, individuals under the age of 18. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has submitted personal information to our Service, please contact us immediately at privacy@humanizeai.app and we will take prompt steps to delete that information.
